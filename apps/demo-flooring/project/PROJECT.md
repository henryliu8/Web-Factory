# Project

Grain & Ground is a fictional Sydney flooring studio used to demonstrate a content-rich Web Factory project. The site helps homeowners and interior designers compare natural flooring materials and arrange a consultation.

## Goals

- Present timber, stone, and wool through an editorial, material-led experience.
- Keep page copy and repeatable records in Astro Content Collections.
- Demonstrate project-owned Header, Footer, HomeSlider, styles, and service routes.
- Preserve the Web Factory boundary: Project > Theme > Template > Shared.

All names, addresses, contact details, testimonials, and service descriptions are demonstration content.
